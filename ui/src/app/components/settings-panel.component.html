Privacy

Two sensitive features, off by default. Everything stays on this Mac — nothing here is ever sent to a server.

Keep audio Store each meeting's audio locally to replay later. Off, audio is discarded after transcription.
Remember speakers Store voiceprints so future meetings auto-recognize people you've named. Off, no voiceprints are stored or matched.
Sync audio Allow a future Hark sync to carry stored audio to your other Macs. Today audio never leaves this device.
Sync speakers Allow a future Hark sync to carry voiceprints to your other Macs. Today voiceprints never leave this device.

What Hark stores, and where

  • · Transcripts → your vault, as plain markdown. Always (it's the product).
  • · Audio → only if Keep audio is on, in your vault.
  • · Voiceprints → only if Remember speakers is on, in vault/.speakers/ — local, gitignored, never networked.

Recording and storing voiceprints may require consent under your local laws — you're responsible for obtaining it. Hark keeps everything on your Mac to support that. This isn't legal advice.

What leaves your Mac

A cloud model receives transcript text only — never your audio or voiceprints — and only when you invoke an action (Ask, summarize). What's sent is redacted and logged locally so you can see it. A local model sends nothing off this Mac.
