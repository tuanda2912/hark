Two things, off by default.

Both store sensitive data on this Mac. We keep them off until you turn them on — and you can change either anytime in Settings → Privacy.

Keep audio
Store each meeting's audio locally so you can replay and verify-by-ear later. Off, audio is discarded after transcription.
Remember speakers
Store voiceprints locally so future meetings auto-recognize people you've named. Off, no voiceprints are stored or matched.
Recording and storing voiceprints may require consent under your local laws — you're responsible for obtaining it. Hark keeps everything on your Mac to support that. This isn't legal advice.
